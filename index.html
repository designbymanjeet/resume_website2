About

I have been designing brands since March 2024. I started as a freelancer, making brand identities, logo systems, guidelines, packaging and digital creatives.

In July 2025 I co-founded Hawkfin, a branding and marketing agency, and ran its design work. In March 2026 I joined Flicker Tech Consulting as a designer and associate brand manager.

I care about more than how a brand looks. I want it to feel the same everywhere people meet it.

In August 2026 I started my BBA at IIM Bangalore. Outside design, I play badminton at All India level.
